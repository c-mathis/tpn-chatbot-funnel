Understanding Your Options

Installment Agreement

A payment plan that allows you to pay your tax debt over time in manageable monthly amounts.

  • Typically stops collection actions
  • Various plan types available
  • Based on your ability to pay

Offer in Compromise

An agreement with the IRS to settle your tax debt for less than the full amount you owe.

  • Based on ability to pay
  • Requires IRS approval
  • Not everyone qualifies

Currently Not Collectible

A status that temporarily pauses IRS collection when you're experiencing financial hardship.

  • Provides temporary relief
  • Requires documentation
  • Periodically reviewed by IRS
